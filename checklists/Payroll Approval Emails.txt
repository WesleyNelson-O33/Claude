E1  Thursday, advance notice
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SEND:     Thursday, 9am, before the period closes
TO:       All approving managers
CC:       Graham, Duncan, accounts
SUBJECT:  Action required: timesheet approvals due 10am Monday [DD Mon] - fortnight ending [DD Mon]

Hi all,

The current pay fortnight closes this Friday [DD Mon]. All timesheets for the period [DD Mon]
to [DD Mon] must be approved in Employment Hero by 10am Monday [DD Mon].

When you approve a timesheet you are confirming, for each person in your team:

  - The hours recorded are the hours actually worked
  - The actual times reconcile to the roster, or the difference is accounted for
  - Any overtime was authorised in advance by you, and the approval is attached to the timesheet
    showing the reason, whether it is chargeable to the client, and whether it is being taken as
    pay or as time in lieu
  - Any time in lieu accrued or taken is correct against the roster
  - Meal breaks are recorded accurately, including where a break was not taken
  - The cost centre on each shift is correct

Payroll processes what has been approved. Payroll does not check hours against the roster and
does not decide whether overtime was authorised; those checks sit with you as the approver.

Any time that is not approved by 10am Monday will be paid in the next pay run. It will not be
estimated or entered manually, and the pay run will not be held while approvals are chased.

If you will be away on Monday morning, please either approve before you go or tell me now who
is approving on your behalf.

Thanks,

[Name]

E7  Announcing the change, once
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SEND:     Once, at least one full fortnight before it takes effect
TO:       All approving managers and department heads
CC:       accounts
SUBJECT:  Change to payroll timesheet approvals, effective fortnight ending [DD Mon]

All,

From the fortnight ending [DD Mon] we are formalising how timesheets are approved. The change
is about who checks what; the deadlines and the systems are unchanged.

Approving managers are responsible for confirming, before approving a timesheet:

  - The hours recorded were actually worked
  - The actual times reconcile to the roster, or the difference is understood and accepted
  - Any overtime was authorised in advance, with the approval attached to the timesheet recording
    the reason, whether it is chargeable to the client, and whether it is paid or taken as time in
    lieu
  - Any time in lieu accrued or taken is correct against the roster
  - Meal breaks are recorded accurately
  - The cost centre on each shift is correct

Payroll is responsible for processing approved timesheets correctly: applying the award and the
overtime matrix, verifying pay rates, balancing the pay run, and superannuation, PAYG and
reporting. Payroll will not check hours against rosters and will not decide whether overtime or
time in lieu was authorised. Those checks require knowledge of what was worked, which sits with
the manager, not with payroll.

Two practical consequences:

  - Timesheets must be approved by 10am on the Monday of the processing week. Any time not approved
    by then will be paid in the next pay run. It will not be entered manually and the pay run will
    not be held.
  - Where payroll finds something that looks wrong on an approved timesheet, it will come back to
    the approving manager as a query rather than being corrected by payroll.

Please make sure a delegate is set up in Employment Hero for any period you are away, so
approvals are not held up by leave.

[Duncan / Graham]
